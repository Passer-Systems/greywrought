# Native parchment HUD art

Icons are restored unchanged from Greywrought commit
`afbb56565da9b4e0d4590da8f9396e356fe25711`, with their original source note in
greywrought:assets/ui/icons/SOURCE.md. Only the displayed subset is included.

The parchment and decorative local-map drawings were authored for this HUD.
Their SVG originals accompany the PNGs used by Bevy. Map marks are live game
positions; the underlying illustrated trees and pond are decorative.

Liberation Serif Regular 2.1.5 is bundled unchanged under its SIL Open Font
License in greywrought:assets/ui/fonts/LICENSE.
